## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the primal luxury of live-fire cooking, slow-cured smoke, and meticulous culinary craftsmanship. It is tailored for discerning epicures, meat connoisseurs, and hospitality seekers who value provenance, ancient wood-fire technique, and uncompromising gastronomic refinement.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile / Warm Atmospheric Depth**:
- **Primal & Refined**: Raw iron, charred timber, and searing embers translated into structured, modern digital layouts.
- **Editorial Poise**: High-contrast, literary typographic pacing reminiscent of bespoke culinary quarterlies and Michelin-starred tasting menus.
- **Micro-Atmospherics**: Deep cast-iron blacks accented by delicate glowing ember delineations, smoke-diffused overlays, and subdued brass and bone details.

## Colors
The palette is grounded in the chromatic life cycle of timber, hearth, and white-hot fire:

- **Ember & Flame (Primary & Secondary)**: `#FF5E1E` (Live Ember) serves as the primary action spark and focal highlight, while `#E04006` (Char Ember) drives hover states and emphasized accents. `#D97706` (Flame Amber) provides warmth for badges, accolades, and secondary culinary tags.
- **Cast Iron & Hearth (Neutrals)**: Built from an ultra-deep black-brown continuum. `#0E0C0B` anchors the base canvas, `#14100E` defines elevated surface layers, and `#1F1916` provides card backgrounds with subtle warm cast-iron undertones.
- **Bone Ivory (Typography & Lineage)**: High-legibility, organic light tones avoiding sterile cool whites. `#F5EBE1` handles primary editorial titles and body text; `#D1C5B8` (Ash Bone) carries secondary metadata and descriptions; `#786B63` (Smoked Charcoal) handles disabled states and subtle borders.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the pacing between editorial grandeur and culinary precision:
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys timeless epicurean tradition, authority, and high-heat passion. Use italic styling sparingly for cuts, wood varietals (e.g., *Post Oak Smoked*), or vintage dates.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, sharp, and highly legible against deep carbon backgrounds. Ensures menus, allergen tags, preparation timings, and reservation controls remain frictionless.
- **Case Conventions**: Section eyebrows and culinary certifications leverage `label-sm` or `label-md` in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.1em` to `0.14em`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`>1024px`), an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`<768px`).

- **Outer Margins & Hero Space**: Desktop screens use generous 3rem (`margin`) canvas margins to evoke the breathing room of an open hearth room, collapsing to 1.25rem (`margin-mobile`) on handheld viewports.
- **Rhythm & Pairings**: Tightly related menu specs (weight, dry-age duration, price) use `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Card internals scale from `space-md` to `space-lg`. Large thematic transitions between grill philosophy, cellar selections, and booking engines leverage `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics the natural ambient light radiating from a low charcoal fire in a dark dining room:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas starts at `#0E0C0B`. Interactive cards and panels sit on `#161210` (Surface Tier 1), floating modals and flyout booking engines occupy `#1E1715` (Surface Tier 2).
- **Ember Glow Borders**: Instead of harsh bright lines, cards utilize an ultra-subtle border tinted with orange-red hues (`rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.14)`), shifting to a focused ember flare (`rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.45)`) on hover or active status.
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are warm-tinted and deeply diffused:
  - Base Card: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(6, 4, 3, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.08)`
  - Elevated Overlay / Menu Drawer: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.92), 0 0 40px -10px rgba(224, 64, 6, 0.12)`

## Shapes
A disciplined, architectural shape system reflecting heavy steel cutting blocks and hand-forged grill iron:
- **Base Element Radius**: Minimal 0.25rem (`4px`) roundedness maintains clean, masculine lines without sharp discomfort.
- **Larger Cards & Modal Framing**: Scaled up slightly to `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / `8px`) to contain menu grids and imagery.
- **Badges & Micro-tags**: Kept at 0.25rem for an artisanal, stamped wax-seal or burnished iron look. Pill buttons are strictly prohibited to avoid playful or casual dilution.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Ember Fire)**: Background `#FF5E1E`, text `#0E0C0B`, weight 600. On hover, background shifts to `#E04006` with an outer amber glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Smoked Iron)**: Background `#1F1916`, text `#F5EBE1`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.2)`. On hover, border color warms to `#FF5E1E` with text `#FF5E1E`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost Hearth)**: Text `#F5EBE1`, no background. Underline accent glows ember on hover.

### Culinary Cards (Cuts, Cellar & Dishes)
- **Background**: `#161210` with a fine 1px border of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.
- **Image Integration**: Deep contrast photography with natural vignetting melting seamlessly into the `#161210` body.
- **Details Section**: Cut specification (e.g., "45-Day Dry Aged Prime Bone-in Ribeye"), pricing formatted in tabular `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and wood pairing note set in italic `Playfair Display`.

### Badges & Provenance Seals
- **Style**: Compact, uppercase tracking with `label-sm`. Background `rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.08)` with solid border `1px solid rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.3)`. Text rendered in `#F97316`.
- **Use Cases**: "A5 Miyazaki Wagyu", "Post Oak Slow-Smoked 16hrs", "Chef's Cut".

### Form Inputs & Reservation Fields
- **Container**: Dark iron surface (`#120F0D`), text `#F5EBE1`, placeholder `#786B63`.
- **Border**: Subtle ash gray `rgba(209, 197, 184, 0.15)`. On focus, transitions to `#FF5E1E` border with a 2px ember-diffused halo.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Frame**: Squared, 4px corner radius, `#1F1916` base. Checked state displays solid `#FF5E1E` with cast-iron `#0E0C0B` checkmark/indicator.

### Menu List Row
- **Divider**: 1px dashed `rgba(209, 197, 184, 0.12)`.
- **Layout**: Left-aligned dish name and botanical/smoke infusion notes; right-aligned weight and price. Prices highlighted in `#F5EBE1`.
